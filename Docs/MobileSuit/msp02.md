# MSP-02 Late-UC Mobile Suit

An original late-UC suit in the ν's colour family (white armour, dark navy chest and inner panels,
yellow vents, red accents, grey frame), sized and powered after published late-UC specs.

| | |
|---|---|
| Head height | ~21.2 m |
| Mass | 30 t dry + 10 t propellant + 2.6 t rifle + 3.8 t shield ≈ 46.4 t |
| Main thrust | 4 × 179.5 kN backpack + 2 × 120.6 kN leg (= 4 × 18,300 + 2 × 12,300 kgf) |
| Verniers | 18 × 25–35 kN (shoulders, chest, skirts, feet) |
| Mains layout | backpack bells at the bottom of the pods, firing down and 25° back (gimbal 28°) |

regenerate, or tweak the scene directly in the editor.

## Rig

Rigid mech rig: one mesh per part, weighted 100% to one bone, so it can be animated or re-rigged
without re-weighting.

`root, pelvis, spine, chest, neck, head, backpack, hatch_upper, hatch_lower`, and per side (`.L/.R`)
`shoulder, upper_arm, forearm, hand, skirt_front, skirt_side, skirt_rear, thigh, shin, foot`.

## Equipment and poses

Equipment is separate (`Entities/Equipment/`) and mounted at runtime by `MsLoadout` on a
`BoneAttachment3D`, using `mount_transform` (body frame at rest).

| Key | Equipment | Pose (layer) | Occupies |
|-----|-----------|--------------|----------|
| 1 | Beam rifle | `rifle_ready`: right arm raised to the aim line | `arm_r` |
| 2 | Shield | `shield_guard`: left forearm across the body, face forward | `arm_l` |

While an arm is occupied it's excluded from AMBAC, and the legs and waist take over balancing.
Layers are blended over `idle` with bone filters, and AMBAC offsets are added last by a
`SkeletonModifier3D`.
